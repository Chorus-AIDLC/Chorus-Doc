Once a workspace holds more than a handful of ideas, proposals, tasks, and documents, you
need two ways to move: jump straight to something you can name, and step back to see how a
project fits together. This guide covers both — **global search** and the project
**resource graph**. For what each covers conceptually, follow the reference links inline.

## Jump to anything with global search

Global search finds any entity across your workspace and doubles as fast navigation. Open it
from the search bar in the sidebar, or press **⌘K** / **Ctrl+K** from anywhere. Start typing
and results appear as you type; selecting one takes you straight to that entity.

Two controls narrow what you see:

- **Scope** — search the whole workspace, one group, or just the current project. Chorus
  defaults to a sensible scope for wherever you are.
- **Type filter** — limit results to tasks, ideas, proposals, documents, or projects.

## See how a project fits together with the graph

When you want the shape of a whole project rather than a single item, open its **Graph** tab
— a mind-map of the project's ideas, proposals, tasks, and documents and the links between
them.

- **Expand** and **collapse** to move between the big picture and the details: an idea opens
  to its proposals, and a proposal to its tasks and documents.
- **Filter** node types to focus on just ideas, proposals, tasks, or documents.
- **Zoom** and fit the view to frame what you're looking at, and search for a specific node
  by name.
- Click any node to open that entity in a side panel without leaving the graph.

For what the node types and edge kinds mean and how the graph is laid out, see the
[resource graph reference](/reference/resource-graph/).
