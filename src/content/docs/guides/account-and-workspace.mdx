This guide covers everything around your account: signing in, choosing between workspaces,
the first-run onboarding wizard, the language and theme controls, and signing out. If you
are installing Chorus on your own machine for the first time, start with
[Get started](/guides/getting-started/) — it covers the local email/password install, and
this page does not repeat it.

## Sign in

Enter your email or local username and select **Continue**. Chorus discovers the available sign-in methods before asking for a password:

- **Local workspace account.** Use the username or email from your invitation. Choose the workspace if prompted, then enter your password. Usernames and emails are case-insensitive.
- **Single sign-on (SSO).** Use your email and complete authentication with your workspace's identity provider.
- **Default Auth.** The single account configured with `DEFAULT_USER` and `DEFAULT_PASSWORD` uses its email and deployment password. This is separate from managed local workspace accounts.
- **Multiple identities.** Choose the intended local workspace, SSO workspace, default account, or Super Admin identity. Configuring a default account does not bypass this choice.

A local workspace uses local accounts, not OIDC alongside them. The same email may still have separate identities in other workspaces. For deployment setup, see [Super Admin and authentication setup](/guides/authentication-setup/).

### Accept an invitation or reset a password

Open the link your administrator shares privately and set a password of at least 8 characters and at most 72 UTF-8 bytes. Links expire after 24 hours, work once, and are replaced when a new link is issued. Chorus does not email them automatically. Completing the form does not sign you in: return to sign-in and use your username or email and new password. Ask your Workspace Admin for a fresh invitation or reset link if needed; there is no public registration or self-service password reset.

## Choose a workspace

If only several SSO workspaces match, the chooser lists their names and identity providers. If local accounts or other sign-in methods also match, use the identity picker instead. Verify the workspace before entering a password; matching usernames or emails in different workspaces do not represent one shared account. You can go back and change the identifier.

## First-run onboarding

The first time you enter a fresh workspace, Chorus opens a six-step onboarding
wizard. You can skip it and finish later. It walks you through:

1. **Welcome** — a look at the pipeline your work travels through:
   **Idea → Proposal → Task → Execute → Verify → Done**.
2. **Create an agent** — name an AI agent and choose its permissions; Chorus generates an
   API key for it. (Full agent setup lives in
   [Prepare agent access](/guides/administrator-setup/) — this is the quick inline version.)
3. **Copy the API key** — save it somewhere safe. It is shown only once.
4. **Install** — choose your agent client (Claude Code, Codex, Kiro, OpenCode, OpenClaw, or
   others) and follow the setup steps shown for it.
5. **Test** — Chorus waits until your agent connects, then confirms.
6. **Done** — a setup summary, with shortcuts to your projects and settings.

You can reopen the wizard any time from **Settings**.

:::note[What the agent does]
The **Create an agent** and **Test** steps set up and then verify an AI agent identity. Once
you create the agent and install its client, the agent connects to Chorus on its own; the
**Test** step just watches for that connection.
:::

For how each client platform connects and behaves, see the [agent platforms reference](/reference/agents/).

## Display name and avatar

Open **Settings** to edit your own profile. These controls are for signed-in users, including local, OIDC, and Default Auth users, not agent API keys or the Super Admin panel.

- **Display name:** enter 1–32 Unicode code points after trimming surrounding whitespace. Emoji are supported; a composed emoji may count as more than one. Select **Save** or press Enter outside text composition. **Cancel** discards the draft. This changes your visible name, not your login username or email; a customized name is not overwritten by later OIDC sign-ins.
- **Avatar:** select **Randomize** to preview a generated avatar. Repeat until satisfied, then select **Save**. **Cancel** or leaving the page discards the unsaved preview. This is a generated avatar selector, not an image-upload field.

Typing or randomizing alone never saves. Wait for confirmation; a failed save keeps your previous saved profile and lets you retry. Name and avatar save independently. Saved changes update the current account display and persist after reload; they do not edit another workspace identity.

## Language and theme

Your display preferences live in the sidebar footer.

- **Theme** — choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** (which follows your operating
  system).
- **Language** — choose **English**, **中文**, **한국어**, or **日本語**, and the interface
  switches immediately. You can also change the language from the **Settings** page.

## Sign out

The sidebar footer has a **Sign out** button. Selecting it ends your session and returns you
to the sign-in screen, where you can sign in again with the same or a different email.
